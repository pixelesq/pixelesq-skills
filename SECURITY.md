# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@pixelesq.com with what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the host it affects (mcp.pixelesq.app, pixelesq-mcp.pixelesq.workers.dev, or www.pixelesq.com). Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## Scope

- The Pixelesq MCP server and its sign-in pages on mcp.pixelesq.app and pixelesq-mcp.pixelesq.workers.dev
- The image upload route used by the in-chat upload box

The same contact is published at /.well-known/security.txt on both connector hosts.
